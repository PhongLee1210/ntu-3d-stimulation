## History: founding, the move, the successive names, and the La San site

### Takeaway
CONFIRMED: founded 01/8/1959 as **Khoa Thủy sản** inside **Học viện Nông Lâm Hà Nội**, split
off as **Trường Thủy sản** 16/8/1966, moved from Hải Phòng to Nha Trang in 1976, and renamed in
turn **Trường Đại học Hải sản** → **Trường Đại học Thủy sản** → **Trường Đại học Nha Trang**
(2006). The campus sits on **đồi La San / Lasan** (La Salle hill) — but the school that stood on
that hill was the La San brothers' monastery/juniorate, **not** Trường La San Bá Ninh, which was
a different, downtown school. The brief's premise needs correcting.

### Inferences
- The brief's "La San Bá Ninh school previously occupied the site" is almost certainly a
  conflation of two La San properties: the **school** Bá Ninh (26 Duy Tân, downtown) and the
  **monastery/juniorate on Đồi La San** in Vĩnh Thọ, which is the campus hill. For game copy, say
  the campus stands on **đồi La San**, named after the La San (De La Salle) brothers who built on
  it from 1932–33 and were moved off the hilltop in 1978 — do **not** say "the Bá Ninh school
  became the university".
- The 1973 move "về thành phố Hải Phòng" in the official timeline implies the 1959–1973 period was
  spent at An Thụy and in Hà Nội, with Hải Phòng only the last northern home before the 1976 move
  south. The 1966 name "Trường Thủy sản" sat under **Tổng cục Thủy sản** per Wikipedia.

### Gaps
- No source found that states directly, in print, "the university took over the La San hilltop
  buildings". The 1978 eviction of the Brothers from the hilltop and the university's arrival in
  1976 are consistent but the handover is not documented in anything I could reach.
- lasan.org (the primary La San archive) refuses HTTPS fetches from this environment (TLS alert);
  its Bá Ninh page would be worth a manual read.

## Current structure: the December 2025 reorganisation, counts, programmes

### Takeaway
CONFIRMED: on **12/12/2025** NTU merged and upgraded its engineering, fisheries and business
faculties into **three constituent schools** (Trường Thủy sản và Khoa học sự sống; Trường Kỹ thuật
và Công nghệ; Trường Kinh tế và Kinh doanh), renamed most administrative offices, and turned the
Library into a centre. Roughly **15,000 students** across about **20 units**, **450 full-time
lecturers** (2025).

## The Museum of Aquatic Plants and Animals (Bảo tàng Thủy sinh vật), A-6

### Takeaway
CONFIRMED and a strong landmark: NTU's own **Bảo tàng Thủy sinh vật** is inside the campus, grew
out of a practical-teaching room of the old Khoa Nuôi trồng thủy sản, is more than 60 years old,
and displays **over 1,100 specimens** of crustaceans, fish, molluscs, marine plants and other
invertebrates/vertebrates from fresh, brackish and salt water. It is **not** the Viện Hải dương
học aquarium at Cầu Đá — that is a separate institution several km away and the two are constantly
confused in search results.

### Cited Findings
- "Nhiều sức hút từ Bảo tàng Thủy sinh vật tại Trường ĐH Nha Trang": the museum is "ngay trong
  chính khuôn viên Nhà trường"; founded over 60 years ago from a practice room of the former
  **Khoa Nuôi trồng thủy sản** (now Viện Nuôi trồng thủy sản); **over 1,100 specimens**, preserved
  in chemicals or kept dry, covering freshwater, brackish and marine habitats. —
  [ntu.edu.vn](https://ntu.edu.vn/tin-tuc/nhieu-suc-hut-tu-bao-tang-thuy-sinh-vat-tai-truong-dh-nha-trang)
- It has a lookup catalogue (danh mục tra cứu mẫu vật) arranged by row and shelf, low to high,
  giving each specimen's local name, scientific name and collection site. —
  [ntu.edu.vn](https://ntu.edu.vn/tin-tuc/nhieu-suc-hut-tu-bao-tang-thuy-sinh-vat-tai-truong-dh-nha-trang)
- Visitors are welcome: high-school students, new students, and people from inside and outside the
  university. Its primary purpose is teaching, practicals and research. —
  [ntu.edu.vn](https://ntu.edu.vn/tin-tuc/nhieu-suc-hut-tu-bao-tang-thuy-sinh-vat-tai-truong-dh-nha-trang)
- The page credits the former lecturer **Lê Thị Hồng Mơ** with reorganising and restoring the old
  specimens. (Only named person tied to the museum in any source I found.) —
  [ntu.edu.vn](https://ntu.edu.vn/tin-tuc/nhieu-suc-hut-tu-bao-tang-thuy-sinh-vat-tai-truong-dh-nha-trang)
- The same page credits **Viện Nuôi trồng thủy sản** as first in Vietnam to artificially breed
  **cá chim vây vàng** (golden pompano), **cá chẽm** (barramundi), **tôm sú** (black tiger shrimp),
  **cá trắm cỏ** (grass carp) and **cá mè hoa** (bighead carp). —
  [ntu.edu.vn](https://ntu.edu.vn/tin-tuc/nhieu-suc-hut-tu-bao-tang-thuy-sinh-vat-tai-truong-dh-nha-trang)
- A **second, distinct campus museum**: the Viện Khoa học và Công nghệ Khai thác Thủy sản has "01
  Phòng bảo tàng ngư cụ (lưu giữ hàng trăm mẫu vật)" — a **fishing-gear museum room** with
  hundreds of items, alongside a gear-making practical room, "01 Phòng mô phỏng điều động tàu hiện
  đại bậc nhất ở Việt Nam", "01 Phòng mô phỏng đánh cá duy nhất tại Việt Nam" (the only fishing
  simulator in Vietnam), a seamanship/marine-electrics room and a chart room. —
  [SOTAY2019](https://tuyensinh.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/2/files/So%20tay%20TS2019%2003042019_edit%201.pdf)
- For contrast, the city's **Viện Hải dương học** (1 Cầu Đá; founded 1922/1923) holds 20,000+
  specimens of 4,000+ species plus live tanks and a reconstructed whale skeleton (sources differ:
  "gần 26 m" per NTU's handbook, "18 m" per a travel source). —
  [SOTAY2019](https://tuyensinh.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/2/files/So%20tay%20TS2019%2003042019_edit%201.pdf);
  [vinpearl.com](https://vinpearl.com/vi/chia-se-kinh-nghiem-tham-quan-vien-hai-duong-hoc-nha-trang-chi-tiet)

### Inferences
- Two museum-like interiors exist on campus and both are game-ready: a jars-and-shelves natural
  history room (Bảo tàng Thủy sinh vật) and a net-and-hook room with a ship-handling simulator
  (phòng bảo tàng ngư cụ + phòng mô phỏng). The simulator rooms are the most distinctive
  "only-in-NTU" interiors mentioned by any source.
- Because the brief's map puts the museum at **A-6**, and A-blocks are the administrative/east side
  of campus, the museum is plausibly near the Viện Nuôi trồng thủy sản's teaching space — but the
  block assignment is from the old map and I could not confirm it against a current source.

### Gaps
- No opening hours, ticket price or booking procedure published anywhere I could find. Do not
  invent any; in game terms, treat it as "open on request / hỏi thầy cô" rather than ticketed.
- No photo-based description of the room's look (lighting, cabinet style) in any text source.
