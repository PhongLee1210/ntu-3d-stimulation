**Scope note:** this is Trường Đại học Nha Trang (NTU), 02 Nguyễn Đình Chiểu, Nha Trang, Khánh Hòa — the former Trường Đại học Thủy sản (Fisheries University). Not NTU Singapore, not NTU Taiwan.

**Method note:** text sources on the Vietnamese web say almost nothing about this campus's architecture. Nearly everything useful below came from *looking at photographs* — chiefly the official photo set on `ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat`, one Wikimedia Commons photo, and two official aerial/news images. Each visual finding names the image it came from and states what is actually visible in it. Inferences are kept separate.

## What does the MAIN BUILDING (A-2 / "Nhà Đa Năng" / administrative building) look like?

### Takeaway
**I could not confirm the existence, name or appearance of a building labelled "A-2" or "Nhà Đa Năng" at NTU.** Neither the official facilities page, the site search, Vietnamese Wikipedia nor any news result mentions those labels. What *is* confirmed from photographs is that the big, sea-facing, signage-bearing landmark block of the campus is **G5** — a long, gently curved, cream-and-ochre slab of about six storeys carrying giant blue roof-top letters reading **ĐẠI HỌC NHA TRANG / NHA TRANG UNIVERSITY**. Treat the A-2 premise as unverified and G5 as the confirmed "face of the university".

### Cited Findings
- The official facilities page (`Cơ sở vật chất`) lists only six categories — location, lecture halls, digital library, dormitory system, sports facilities, laboratories — and **names no administrative/main building and no "nhà đa năng"** — [NTU, Cơ sở vật chất](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat)
- A site search on ntu.edu.vn for "nhà đa năng" returns "Chưa có nội dung nào" (no content) — [ntu.edu.vn search](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/Tim-kiem?q=nh%C3%A0%20%C4%91a%20n%C4%83ng)
- Web searches for `"Nhà Đa Năng" "Đại học Nha Trang"` and for `"Đại học Nha Trang" nhà đa năng A2 khánh thành` returned no page describing such a building.
- **Visible in `giang-duong-g7.jpg`... no — in `giang-duong-g5-dhnt.jpg`** (official, captioned G5): a long building photographed from a raised viewpoint, seen end-on and in perspective, whose **top parapet carries large free-standing capital letters "ĐẠI HỌC NHA TRANG" with a smaller second line "NHA TRANG UNIVERSITY" beneath**. The letters are dark blue/navy, mounted on the parapet edge, not painted on the wall. The facade below is an open, continuously-glazed/louvred corridor front, pale cream, and the building reads as roughly 4–5 visible storeys from this angle. It sits above a path and a flowering bougainvillea arbour. — [NTU G5 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g5-dhnt.jpg)
- **Visible in `lasan_thumb.png`** (official aerial, from the article *Đồi Lasan – nơi những ước mơ vươn ra biển lớn*): the same long block seen from above — it is **gently curved/dog-legged in plan, with a flat roof finished in a pale green/grey sheet or waterproofing**, running parallel to the coast road at the seaward edge of the campus, and the roof-mounted lettering is visible as a line of characters along the parapet facing the sea and the Trần Phú bridge. Immediately inland of it stand several **shorter blocks with pitched, red/terracotta hipped roofs** and cream walls. — [NTU news image 12325](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/56/images/news/12325/img/doi-lasan-noi-nhung-uoc-mo-vuon-ra-bien-lon_thumb400.jpg)
- **Visible in `giang-duong-g2-dhnt.jpg`** (official, captioned G2): a **free-standing slab block of 5 occupied storeys over an open ground floor (6 levels total)**, photographed against the sea at sunrise. Walls are **warm ochre/yellow with white horizontal floor bands**; the long elevation is an **open access gallery (hành lang) behind a continuous white balustrade**, so the facade reads as alternating deep shadow and white bands. The **narrow gable end is painted red/terracotta with a grid of small square windows**. The roof is **flat concrete with a small red-painted parapet/plant room**. It stands on the slope directly above the bay with Hòn Chồng / the northern headlands and fishing boats behind it. — [NTU G2 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g2-dhnt.jpg)

### Inferences
- The "recognisable face of the university" described in the brief as A-2 + B-3 is, on the photographic evidence, **the G5 signage block plus the red-hip-roofed cluster immediately behind it**. The long sea-facing slab with roof letters is what appears in essentially every official aerial.
- The brief's "large red/terracotta-roofed complex at the south of campus" is consistent with the red-hip-roofed low blocks visible inland of G5 in `lasan_thumb.png` and with the tiled arcade in the 2019 drone still — but I cannot tie them to the code "A-2".

### Gaps
- No source found that uses the building codes A-1/A-2/B-3 or the term "Nhà Đa Năng" for NTU. The official campus-map page (`/gioi-thieu/ban-do-truong-dai-hoc-nha-trang`) renders its map via a script and exposed **no image URL** to scrape, so the code-to-building key could not be recovered.
- Storey count, entrance portico, symmetry, clock or tower on the putative A-2: **unknown**. No clock tower is visible in any photograph I inspected.

## Most photographed / most recognisable structure or view on campus

### Takeaway
On the available evidence the signature image is **the aerial of the campus sitting on its green headland between Nha Trang Bay and the city, with the roof-top "ĐẠI HỌC NHA TRANG" lettering on the long sea-facing block** — not a gate, dome or tower. The second strongest candidate is the **dolphin fountain in the G8 courtyard**.

### Cited Findings
- The official "Cơ sở vật chất" page's lead claim is positional, not architectural: NTU "có vị trí đẹp nhất so với các trường đại học khác tại Việt Nam", surrounded by Nha Trang Bay; main campus 20 ha — [NTU, Cơ sở vật chất](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat)
- The university's annual report describes the campus as sitting on **đồi Lasan (La San hill) in the north-east of the city, with two sides facing the sea** — [NTU 2025 annual report](https://phongdbcl.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/47/CONG%20KHAI%20NAM%202025-2026/285-BC%20765.BC%20%C4%90HNT%20ngay%2026.6.26%20Bao%20cao%20thuong%20nien%20nam%202025.pdf)
- **Visible in `khuan-vien-dh-nha-trang.JPG`** (the official hero aerial): the campus occupies a low green promontory; from the air one sees a **running track and green sports pitch**, a **large pale shed-roofed hall** (the gymnasium) beside it, several **long blocks with bright red/terracotta pitched roofs** in the middle of the site, and lower white blocks with **green/teal pitched roofs**, all embedded in heavy tree cover, with the high-rises of Nha Trang and the bay behind. — [NTU campus aerial](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/khuan-vien-dh-nha-trang.JPG)
- A Vietnamese photo-essay about NTU is built entirely around **hundred-plus old trees more than half a century old, sea-and-"forest" campus**, and mentions building **G7** by name as a photogenic spot (paper-flower/bougainvillea path) — it describes no monument or gate — [danchuphapluat.vn photo essay](https://danchuphapluat.vn/choang-ngop-voi-hang-cay-co-thu-co-tuoi-doi-hon-nua-the-ky-tai-ngoi-truong-vua-co-rung-vua-co-bien-5792.html)
- **Visible in `yt_6QuWR_ntnJQ.jpg`** (official drone still, 26/03/2019, NTU 60th anniversary): the **main ceremonial courtyard** — a large paved square in **red/terracotta brick pavers with a chequerboard of lighter slabs**, hundreds of students doing mass exercise, a red inflatable arch, flanked on one side by a **three-storey white block with tall narrow bright-blue windows and a slogan board reading "HIỀN TÀI LÀ NGUYÊN KHÍ QUỐC GIA"** over a flat entrance canopy, and on the other side by a **long low arcade/cloister with a deep terracotta-tiled pitched roof on plain square columns**. — [YouTube thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/6QuWR_ntnJQ/maxresdefault.jpg), video linked from [NTU video tour page](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/tham-quan-truong-qua-video)

### Inferences
- For a low-poly model, the single most legible "hero" composition is: **green headland + flat-roofed curved slab with roof letters facing the sea + red-tiled hip roofs behind + gym shed and sports pitch to one side.**
- The ceremonial courtyard with its terracotta pavers and tiled cloister is the emotional centre an alumnus would recognise (it is where the 60th-anniversary events were filmed).

### Gaps
- I found no ranking or statement in any source about which view is "most photographed"; the above is my reading of which images the university itself reuses.

## Marine / fisheries-heritage monument, statue or sculpture

### Takeaway
**Yes — confirmed. A circular fountain with a sculpture of leaping dolphins stands in a courtyard in front of building G8.** A second, different figurative statue (a standing female figure holding up a torch/flame, on a lotus-shaped pink pedestal in a round pool) stands in front of **G3**. No lighthouse, ship mast or anchor was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Visible in `giang-duong-g8-dhnt.jpg`** (official, captioned G8): a **large circular fountain basin ringed by a low white picket/tube railing**, paved apron around it, water jetting high. At its centre is a **dark metal (bronze- or black-painted) sculptural group of several stylised dolphins arching up out of the water, tails down, forming a ring/bouquet**. Behind it stands a **two-storey white building with an open colonnaded gallery on both levels (plain square white columns, simple railing) under a bold red/terracotta pitched tile roof with overhanging eaves**, with blue-green doors/windows. In the mid-ground left there is also a **red-painted arched/ring steel feature** (a low decorative arch over a path). — [NTU G8 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g8-dhnt.jpg)
- **Visible in `motgoc.jpg`** (Wikimedia Commons, 2009): in front of G3, a **round shallow pool with a pale blue painted floor and a low concrete rim**, and at its centre on a **pink/rose lotus-petal pedestal** a **white female figure, standing, one arm raised holding a flame/torch, with what reads as a dolphin or fish form at her feet**. — [Commons: MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg)
- A 2012 internal NTU quality-assurance document is titled "Lễ cắt băng khánh thành bia đá" (ribbon-cutting for a **stone stele**), associated in search results with Norwegian support — the PDF's text streams are compressed and its body could not be read, so the stele's location, material and inscription are unconfirmed — [NTU document](https://phongdbcl.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/47/files/old/Tai%20lieu%20Minh%20chung/TIEUCHUAN%208/Minhchung82/2012/82-12%20Le%20cat%20bang%20khanh%20thanh%20bia%20da.pdf)
- **Visible in `giang-duong-g1.JPG`**: a **dark upright stone monolith/stele, roughly 2–2.5 m tall, rough-hewn, standing on a small plinth in the landscaped terrace below G1**, next to a palm. This is plausibly the 2012 stele but the photo is not captioned. — [NTU G1 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/giang-duong-g1.JPG)

### Inferences
- The dolphin fountain is the campus's explicit marine-heritage sculpture and is an excellent low-poly landmark: circle of water + white railing ring + 4–6 arcing dolphin forms.

### Gaps
- No anchor, ship, mast or lighthouse monument found in any image or text.
- The G3 figure's identity and the stele's inscription are unknown.

## The LIBRARY (Thư viện)

### Takeaway
Only the **interior** is documented by the official image, and it decisively confirms a **round/drum-shaped reading hall**: a circular double-height void with a ring-shaped mezzanine balcony. The exterior was not obtainable, but the interior geometry makes the "rounded/drum-shaped wing" in the brief very likely correct.

### Cited Findings
- **Visible in `thu-vien-dh-nha-trang.jpeg`** (official library photo): a **two-level reading hall built around a large circular opening in the floor slab** — the near edge is a sweeping arc of white balustrade/parapet with a steel handrail, with readers at desks around it on the upper level; below, the lower floor is filled with radially/orthogonally arranged **pale grey-white open metal book stacks**. Structure is **round white concrete columns**; the upper walls are a continuous band of **tall rectangular windows with pale turquoise/teal glazing or blinds**; the ceiling is flat white with **suspended ceiling fans**; the lower floor is laid with **pale green/mint tile**. Individual study carrels have dark grey curved privacy hoods. — [NTU library photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/thu-vien-dh-nha-trang.jpeg)
- The facilities page's library section is headed **"Thư viện số" (digital library)** — [NTU, Cơ sở vật chất](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat)

### Inferences
- A cylindrical/drum volume of at least two tall storeys, with a continuous band of tall windows under a flat roof, white or cream render, teal-tinted glazing. This matches the brief's TV-2/TV-3/TV-4 "wing with a rounded/drum-shaped end".

### Gaps
- Library exterior: storeys, roof form, external colour — **not confirmed**. Codes TV-2/TV-3/TV-4 appear in no source I found.

## Typical lecture-hall (G) blocks — the repeating campus mass

### Takeaway
Confirmed, and richer than "generic Vietnamese institutional slab". NTU's G-blocks are **4–6 storey reinforced-concrete blocks with open single-loaded access galleries**, **flat roofs**, **cream/off-white walls with ochre or brick-red accent planes**, and **strong blue window joinery** — plus two genuinely distinctive formal moves: a **row of barrel/arched roof vaults on G3** and a **full-height rounded drum stair-tower with spiralling balconies on G7**.

### Cited Findings — per block
- **G1** (`giang-duong-g1.JPG`): a **5-storey block over an open/recessed ground floor**. The left half of the facade is clad in a **deep brick-red / terracotta panel field** punctuated by a regular grid of **small square windows with blue frames** (roughly 8 bays × 4 storeys); the right half is **white/pale cream with full-width ribbon glazing** in dark frames. A **tall blank white stair/lift tower projects above the roofline at the junction of the two halves**, acting as a vertical accent. The roof is **flat**, with an open pergola/louvre frame over the top floor of the right-hand wing. Floor levels are expressed as **continuous white horizontal bands/sunshade fins**. A blue signage strip runs across the facade at second-floor level, and the block code "G1" sits on a small blue plate. Foreground: a landscaped terrace with blue-grey concrete benches and a stone path. Sampled pixel in the red panel (shaded): **#684849**; sunlit cream band: **~#c7c9c6** — both are rough single-pixel samples and should be read as "dark brick-red" and "warm off-white". — [NTU G1 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/giang-duong-g1.JPG)
- **G2** (`giang-duong-g2-dhnt.jpg`): described above — **6 levels, ochre-yellow with white floor bands, open gallery behind white balustrade, red-painted gable end with small square window grid, flat roof**, sited on the slope directly over the bay. — [NTU G2 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g2-dhnt.jpg)
- **G3** (`motgoc.jpg`, Commons, high-resolution): the most informative single image.
  - **Four levels**, stepping back as it rises, cream/ivory render throughout.
  - **Ground floor**: deep recessed arcade with **blue double doors** and **green-painted** panels.
  - **First floor**: an open gallery whose balustrade is a **run of white triangular/zig-zag concrete fins** — a very characteristic 1960s–70s detail.
  - **Second floor**: an open corridor behind square white columns; the left bay is closed by a **large white perforated breeze-block / punched-circle screen panel** (a classic Vietnamese *gạch bông gió* sunscreen), with the block code **"G3" on a blue plate** mounted on it.
  - **Third floor**: a deep white **cantilevered slab/eyebrow** shades a run of **ribbon windows with strong blue frames and blue security grilles**.
  - **Roof level**: a **penthouse storey crowned by four large semicircular barrel vaults / arched shells in white**, with arched and rectangular windows beneath them, and a **red-brick-faced service stack/chimney at the right end**. This arched roofline is the single most distinctive silhouette element on the campus.
  - Foreground: the round pool and white torch-bearing statue described above; palms and clipped hedges; a blue regulations noticeboard; concrete apron and asphalt drive.
  — [Commons: MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg)
- **G5** (`giang-duong-g5-dhnt.jpg`): long, slightly curved, pale cream, **continuously glazed/louvred corridor facade**, flat roof carrying the **free-standing navy capital letters "ĐẠI HỌC NHA TRANG" over "NHA TRANG UNIVERSITY"** on the parapet. Approached along a path under a **steel bougainvillea arbour**. — [NTU G5 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g5-dhnt.jpg)
- **G7** (`giang-duong-g7.jpg`): **four to five storeys**, white/cream render, and its corner is a **full-height cylindrical drum** — each floor's balcony sweeps around the drum as a **curved white slab with a thin steel railing**, so the elevation reads as a stack of white rings. Behind the drum the block continues as a straight wing with the same curved-ended balconies. **Blue-framed windows** and a **blue "G7" plate** on the drum at first-floor level. Flat roof, with the drum's parapet continuing the curve. Heavily screened by frangipani (white flowers) and a conifer. — [NTU G7 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g7.jpg)
- **G8** (`giang-duong-g8-dhnt.jpg`): the outlier — **only two storeys**, long and low, **open colonnaded galleries on both levels** with plain square white columns and simple railings, **white walls**, **blue-green / teal doors**, and a **bold red-terracotta pitched tile roof with projecting eaves** and a simple ridge. Sampled roof pixel (sunlit): **#d49b8a**; wall: **~#d0d0d0** — rough samples; read as "warm terracotta" and "white". Fronted by the dolphin fountain. — [NTU G8 photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/giang-duong/giang-duong-g8-dhnt.jpg)
- **Numbers**: NTU has **8 lecture-hall blocks ("8 khu nhà giảng đường") with 120 classrooms across 15,000 m², seating 6,000 students simultaneously**; 100% fitted with modern teaching equipment — [NTU, Cơ sở vật chất](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat)
- Wikipedia (vi) gives **95 classrooms (phòng học)**, which conflicts with the official 120 — [Wikipedia (vi)](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i_h%E1%BB%8Dc_Nha_Trang); prefer the university's own figure, and note the discrepancy.

### Inferences
- A reusable low-poly "G-block" kit: a box 4–6 storeys tall, flat roof, long side = open gallery (recessed plane + white horizontal floor bands + thin railing), short side = solid accent plane in brick-red or ochre with a small-square window grid, blue window frames throughout, a projecting blank stair tower. Variants: G3 gets the four barrel vaults on the roof; G7 gets the cylindrical drum corner; G8 is the two-storey red-tile-roof colonnade.
- The repeated vocabulary (breeze-block screens, zig-zag balustrade fins, deep brise-soleil eyebrows, barrel-vault roof pavilions) places the older cores in **tropical/late-modernist 1960s–70s Southern Vietnamese institutional architecture**, not Soviet-bloc and not French colonial.

### Gaps
- G4 and G6 have no official photo.
- Exact storey counts are read off photographs and may be off by one where the ground floor is open or the site slopes.

## Dormitory (K) blocks

### Takeaway
Confirmed form: **long 4-storey slab blocks, flat roof, open single-loaded access gallery, white/pale grey render with blue doors and railings, and a deeply shaded facade made of repeating balcony bays** — a classic Vietnamese ký túc xá slab, but lighter and more open than the ochre-and-blue cliché.

### Cited Findings
- **Visible in `ky-tuc-xa-dh-nha-trang.JPG`** (official dormitory photo): a **four-storey block** photographed in sharp perspective along its long side. The elevation is organised as **a tall narrow bay-rhythm**: each bay is an open loggia/balcony with a **white-painted railing (horizontal bars)**, separated by **plain white vertical piers running the full height**; every second bay is filled above railing level with a **white perforated breeze-block screen**, giving a strongly striped light/dark facade. The **ground floor is a continuous shaded arcade** with **bright blue doors and blue-barred windows**; a flight of concrete steps leads to the central entrance. Walls are **white to pale blue-grey**; the roof is **flat with a plain parapet**. A small shopfront banner at left advertises a campus canteen/café ("CĂN TIN – CÀ PHÊ – SINH VIÊN", red and white). Trees, a tall street lamp, clipped conifer hedging on the opposite kerb, and a further block with a shallow pitched roof in the distance. — [NTU dormitory photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/ky-tuc-xa-dh-nha-trang.JPG)

### Inferences

## Era and style; La San / Fisheries University heritage; what is preserved

### Takeaway
The campus is **not** French colonial and **not** Soviet-institutional. It is a layered site: a **1960s–70s tropical-modernist core** (breeze-block screens, zig-zag balustrades, barrel-vault roof pavilions, red-tiled colonnaded two-storey blocks) overlaid with **2000s–2010s concrete-and-glass teaching blocks** (G1, G5, G7) and a **2010s steel-truss sports hall**. The hill is named for **Jean-Baptiste de La Salle**, and the tiled colonnaded blocks and arcades are the most plausible survivors of the pre-1975 school, but **no source explicitly confirms which buildings are preserved from the La San era**.

### Cited Findings
- The institution began as the **Khoa Thủy sản (Fisheries Faculty) founded 1 August 1959** at the Hanoi Agro-Forestry Institute; became Trường Thủy sản (1966); **moved from Hải Phòng to Nha Trang in 1976** as Trường Đại học Hải sản; Trường Đại học Thủy sản (1981); renamed **Trường Đại học Nha Trang on 25 July 2006** (Decision 172/2006/QĐ-TTg) — [Wikipedia (vi)](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i_h%E1%BB%8Dc_Nha_Trang)
- The campus stands on **đồi Lasan in phường Vĩnh Thọ**, and the hill is **named after the French priest Jean-Baptiste de La Salle** — [search-surfaced sources incl. the NTU photo essay](https://danchuphapluat.vn/choang-ngop-voi-hang-cay-co-thu-co-tuoi-doi-hon-nua-the-ky-tai-ngoi-truong-vua-co-rung-vua-co-bien-5792.html)
- The campus has **hundreds of mature trees over half a century old** and **more than half its 20 ha is green space** — [danchuphapluat.vn photo essay](https://danchuphapluat.vn/choang-ngop-voi-hang-cay-co-thu-co-tuoi-doi-hon-nua-the-ky-tai-ngoi-truong-vua-co-rung-vua-co-bien-5792.html)
- **Visible in `motgoc.jpg` (2009)**: breeze-block sunscreen panels, zig-zag concrete balustrade fins and a four-barrel-vault roof pavilion on G3 — a vocabulary characteristic of 1960s–70s South Vietnamese institutional building, and clearly predating the 2000s blocks. — [Commons: MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MotgoctruongDHNT.jpg)
- **Visible in `yt_6QuWR_ntnJQ.jpg` (2019)**: alongside the ceremonial courtyard, a **long low arcade with a deep terracotta-tiled pitched roof on plain square columns** — the oldest-looking fabric in any image I inspected. — [YouTube thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/6QuWR_ntnJQ/maxresdefault.jpg)
- The university **mission/philosophy is "KHAI PHÓNG – DỤNG HÀNH – PHỤNG SỰ"**, displayed prominently on the site — [ntu.edu.vn](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/)
- There is a **Bảo tàng Thủy sản (Fisheries Museum)** on campus used as a study destination for international students — [NTU news item, homepage listing](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/) (article "Bảo tàng Thủy sản – điểm đến học tập thực tiễn của học viên quốc tế")
- Satellite facilities beyond the 20 ha main campus: **Cam Ranh saltwater aquaculture experimental farm, Ninh Phụng freshwater experimental farm, a shipyard at Phước Đồng, and a Defence & Security Education Centre at Cam Lâm**, totalling over 50 ha — [NTU, Cơ sở vật chất](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/gioi-thieu/co-so-vat-chat)
- The **Viện Nghiên cứu Chế tạo Tàu thủy (Ship Manufacturing Technology Research Institute)**, working with composite materials, is part of the university — [Wikipedia (vi)](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i_h%E1%BB%8Dc_Nha_Trang)

### Inferences
- The **Fisheries Museum** is a strong candidate for a themed interior landmark in a game, and is the clearest institutional expression of the fisheries heritage besides the dolphin fountain.
- Roof colour on campus is **bimodal**: older/lower blocks have **red-terracotta pitched tile**; mid-period blocks have **green/teal pitched metal**; newer tall blocks have **flat concrete roofs**. All three are visible together in `khuan-vien-dh-nha-trang.JPG` and `lasan_thumb.png`.

### Gaps
- Whether any La San Bá Ninh school building survives, and which: **unconfirmed**. The university's own 65-year history PDF (`65N-NTU-1959 - 2024.pdf`) was surfaced repeatedly by search but I did not extract its body text; it is the best next source.

## Recent construction and renovation (2020–2026)

### Takeaway
Very thin evidence. One building was under construction in the undated football-field photo; the university restructured into three constituent schools in February 2026; and a provincial plan contemplates a *new* Khánh Hòa university campus west of Nha Trang after 2030 (a different institution — do not confuse it with NTU).

### Cited Findings
- In **February 2026** NTU established **three constituent schools (trường) from existing faculties** — an organisational, not physical, change — [Wikipedia (vi)](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i_h%E1%BB%8Dc_Nha_Trang)
- **Visible in `san-bong-da-dh-nha-trang.JPG`**: an **unrendered, bare-concrete multi-storey frame still under construction** on the skyline beyond the pitch. The photo is undated. — [NTU football field photo](https://www.ntu.edu.vn/uploads/6/images/gioi-thieu/san-bong-da-dh-nha-trang.JPG)
- A plan to **build a new Khánh Hòa University in the west of Nha Trang after 2030** was reported — this concerns **Đại học Khánh Hòa**, a separate institution — [Lao Động](https://news.laodong.vn/bat-dong-san/xay-moi-dai-hoc-khanh-hoa-ra-phia-tay-nha-trang-sau-nam-2030-1421476.ldo)
